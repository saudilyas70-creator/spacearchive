THE STORY BEHIND THE MISSION

HUMANITY'S FIRST STEPS ON THE MOON.

The Apollo Lunar Module was designed to take astronauts from lunar orbit to the Moon's surface and bring them safely back to the Command Module.

On July 20, 1969, Apollo 11's Lunar Module, named Eagle, landed on the Moon. Neil Armstrong and Buzz Aldrin became the first humans to walk on its surface.

Across the Apollo program, six Lunar Modules successfully landed astronauts on the Moon, making human lunar exploration possible.
