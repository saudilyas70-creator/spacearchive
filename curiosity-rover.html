THE MISSION

AN ENTIRE LABORATORY ON MARS.

Launched on November 26, 2011, NASA's Curiosity rover landed inside Gale Crater on Mars on August 6, 2012.

Its mission was to investigate whether ancient Mars ever had environmental conditions suitable for microbial life.

Equipped with scientific instruments, cameras and a robotic arm, Curiosity continues to explore the Martian landscape and study its geological history.

SCIENTIFIC ACHIEVEMENTS

WHAT CURIOSITY DISCOVERED.

ANCIENT WATER

Found evidence that liquid water once flowed in ancient Martian environments.

ORGANIC MOLECULES

Detected preserved organic molecules in Martian rocks, important clues in the search for life's building blocks.

ANCIENT HABITABILITY

Found evidence that ancient Gale Crater once had conditions capable of supporting microbial life.
