THE STORY BEHIND THE MISSION

A JOURNEY INTO THE UNKNOWN.

In 2003, NASA launched Spirit as part of its Mars Exploration Rover mission. Its destination was the red planet, where it would investigate rocks, soil and the ancient Martian landscape.

After landing in January 2004, Spirit began exploring the surface of Mars. Its discoveries provided evidence that liquid water had once altered rocks on the planet.

Designed to operate for just 90 Martian days, Spirit continued exploring for more than six years before communication was lost in March 2010.
