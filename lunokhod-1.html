LUNOKHOD 1.

Lunokhod 1 was the first successful remote-controlled rover to operate on another celestial body. It explored the lunar surface and transmitted scientific data and panoramic images back to Earth.

THE STORY BEHIND THE MISSION

THE FIRST WHEELS ON ANOTHER WORLD.

Launched by the Soviet Union aboard Luna 17, Lunokhod 1 reached the Moon on November 17, 1970.

Controlled by a team on Earth, the rover travelled across the lunar surface, capturing panoramic images and studying the Moon's soil and surroundings.

Designed for a mission lasting around three lunar days, Lunokhod 1 continued operating for more than ten months, becoming a landmark achievement in robotic exploration.

MISSION HIGHLIGHTS

EXPLORING THE LUNAR SURFACE.

01

FIRST LUNAR ROVER

Lunokhod 1 became the first successful rover to operate on the surface of another celestial body.

02

LUNAR SOIL ANALYSIS

Its instruments examined the Moon's surface and studied the mechanical properties of lunar soil.

03

LASER RANGING

Its reflector helped scientists measure the distance between Earth and the Moon using laser pulses.
